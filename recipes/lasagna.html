Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made with layers of pasta sheets, rich meat sauce, creamy béchamel sauce, and melted cheese. It is baked until golden and bubbling, creating a delicious combination of tender pasta, savory meat, and creamy textures.

Ingredients

  • 9–12 lasagna sheets
  • 500g ground beef
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 400g tomato sauce
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 200g mozzarella cheese
  • 50g parmesan cheese
  • 500ml milk
  • 40g butter
  • 40g flour
  • Salt and black pepper
  • 1 tsp dried oregano
  • 1 tsp basil
  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
  2. Cook the lasagna sheets according to package instructions.
  3. Heat olive oil in a pan and sauté chopped onions and garlic.
  4. Add ground beef and cook until browned.
  5. Add tomato sauce, salt, pepper, oregano, and basil. Simmer for 10 minutes.
  6. Melt butter in a saucepan, add flour, and gradually whisk in milk to make béchamel sauce.
  7. Spread a layer of meat sauce in a baking dish.
  8. Add lasagna sheets, meat sauce, and béchamel sauce in alternating layers.
  9. Repeat the layers until the ingredients are used.
  10. Top with mozzarella and parmesan cheese.
  11. Bake for 35–40 minutes until golden and bubbling.
  12. Let it rest for 10 minutes before slicing and serving.
